- I found the book interesting.
- We consider him a good teacher.
# 🎯 复合宾语 (Complex Object) 全面总结

## 1️⃣ 定义

**复合宾语** = 宾语 (Object) + 宾语补足语 (Object Complement)  
👉 补足语用来说明宾语的 **状态、身份、性质**，或者表示宾语和补足语之间的动作关系。

例句：

- I found the book **interesting**.
    
- We elected him **monitor**.
    
- She made me **cry**.
    

---

## 2️⃣ 常见动词 + 复合宾语 结构

### 🔹 A. 感觉类动词

see, watch, notice, hear, feel, observe

- I saw him **enter** the room.
    
- We heard the baby **crying**.
    

👉 **考点**：宾补动词形式不同

- 原形动词 (I saw him enter) → 整个动作
    
- V-ing (I saw him entering) → 动作正在进行
    
- V-ed (I saw him killed) → 被动
    

---

### 🔹 B. 使役类动词

make, let, have, get, keep, leave

- She made me **angry**.
    
- I had my car **washed**.
    
- Don’t let him **go**.
    

👉 **考点**：

- make/let + 宾语 + 原形动词
    
- get/have + 宾语 + 过去分词（表示使役）
    

---

### 🔹 C. 判断/认定类动词

find, consider, think, believe, prove

- We consider him **a good teacher**.
    
- She found leaving home **a real wrench**.
    
- I believe this book **useful**.
    

👉 **考点**：

- 宾补常用 **形容词/名词** → 表性质、身份
    
- 宾补可用 **V-ing / 不定式** → 表动作
    

---

### 🔹 D. 其他常见动词

elect, appoint, call, name, choose, declare

- They elected him **president**.
    
- The teacher called me **a fool**.
    

---

## 3️⃣ 宾补的常见形式

|宾补形式|示例|功能|
|---|---|---|
|**形容词**|I found the movie **boring**.|说明性质/状态|
|**名词**|We elected Tom **chairman**.|说明身份|
|**不定式 (to do)**|She asked me **to leave**.|表动作|
|**动名词 (doing)**|She found **leaving home** a wrench.|抽象行为|
|**现在分词 (doing)**|I saw him **running**.|表正在进行|
|**过去分词 (done)**|I had my bike **repaired**.|表被动/完成|
|**副词**|He kept me **outside**.|表状态|

---

## 4️⃣ 考研/专八 高频考点

### 📌 考研常考点

1. **find/consider/believe** + O + OC → 判断类结构常见于阅读长难句
    
    - 考点：名词/形容词宾补
        
    - 真题例：Scientists **consider it impossible** to solve the problem immediately.
        
2. **see/hear/watch** + O + doing/done → 强调动作进行/被动
    
    - 考点：考查 V-ing 和 V-ed 区分
        
3. **make/let/have/get** + O + do/done → 常考使役用法
    
    - 考点：区分原形动词 / 过去分词
        

---

### 📌 专八常考点

1. **名词/形容词作宾补** → 考查句意理解
    
    - The judge declared him **guilty**.
        
2. **动词非谓语作宾补** → 强调逻辑主语关系
    
    - She felt her heart **beating fast**.
        
3. **复杂复合宾语** → 阅读长句考点
    
    - The professor considers globalization **to have influenced** cultural identity profoundly.
        

---

## 5️⃣ 高频对比口诀

- **主动进行** → see sb. **doing** sth.
    
- **动作全过程** → see sb. **do** sth.
    
- **被动结果** → have/get sth. **done**
    
- **身份/性质** → find sb./sth. **adj./n.**
    

---

## 6️⃣ 高频真题例句

- 考研例句：  
    _Economists **consider it essential** that the government reduce the deficit._  
    👉 it 作形式宾语，essential 作宾补，that 从句是真宾语。
    
- 专八例句：  
    _The committee **elected him chairman** of the club._  
    👉 elected + 宾语 + 宾补（名词）。
    

---

✅ 总结一句话：  
复合宾语就是 **动词后跟“宾语 + 补足语”**，补足语常用 **形容词 / 名词 / 分词 / 不定式**，考研、专八高频考查 **find/consider/make/see 等动词搭配**，重点是分清 **宾语和补足语的逻辑关系**。
