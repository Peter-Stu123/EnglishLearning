# 高频考点
## 动词后面接的宾语种类
1. **及物动词**：后面必须有宾语，如 **see**, **find**, **know**, **like** 等。
I **saw** him yesterday.
2. **双及物动词**：后面有两个宾语，一个是直接宾语（物），另一个是间接宾语（人）。常见的动词包括 **give**, **send**, **show** 等。
She **gave** me **a gift**.
## 宾语的省略
在某些情况下，宾语可以省略，通常是在对话中，前文已经明确了宾语，后文为了避免重复可以省略。
1. I can’t find my keys. Can you help me (find my keys)?
## 动词接不定式与动名词的区别
有些动词后可以接**不定式**或**动名词**，但含义有所不同。
1. **remember to do**（记得去做某事）---**remember doing**（记得做过某事）
## 宾语从句
动词后接**宾语从句**是考研英语中的常见结构，宾语从句通常由连接词引导，常见的连接词有 **that, if, whether, wh-词** 等。
1. I believe **that he will come**.
2. She asked **whether I could help her**.
# 真题训练
1. In recent years, scholars have been debating whether genetic modification is a viable solution for the world’s food problems. Many believe **that it is a necessary step** to ensure the survival of the human race. However, others argue that **it might pose serious environmental and ethical risks**.
2. If you want to make progress in life, you should focus on what you **believe in**.
3. The professor explained the theories of quantum mechanics, which many students found difficult to understand. However, he emphasized **that learning quantum mechanics was essential** for those pursuing a career in physics.
