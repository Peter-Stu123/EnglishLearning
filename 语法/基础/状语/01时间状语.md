## ⏳什么是时间状语？

**时间状语**用于说明动作或状态发生的**时间**，可以是一个词，也可以是一个短语或从句。  
例如：

- **I met her yesterday.**（yesterday 是时间状语）
    

---

## 🧠 按词类分类的时间状语示例

---

### ① **副词**（Adverbs）

|副词|中文|用法示例|
|---|---|---|
|today|今天|I have no classes today.|
|now|现在|He is eating now.|
|yesterday|昨天|She left yesterday.|
|tomorrow|明天|We'll go tomorrow.|
|soon|很快|The train will arrive soon.|
|recently|最近|I have seen him recently.|
|already|已经|She has already left.|
|yet|还（否/疑）|Have you finished yet?|
|ever|曾经|Have you ever been to Paris?|
|never|从未|I have never tried sushi.|
|just|刚刚|He has just arrived.|
|then|那时|He was working then.|
|lately|近来|She has been tired lately.|

---

### ② **名词 / 名词短语**（Nouns / Noun Phrases）

|名词短语|中文|用法示例|
|---|---|---|
|this morning|今天早上|I saw her this morning.|
|last week|上周|We met last week.|
|next year|明年|They will graduate next year.|
|two days ago|两天前|He left two days ago.|
|the day before yesterday|前天|It rained the day before yesterday.|
|the day after tomorrow|后天|I'll see you the day after tomorrow.|

---

### ③ **介词短语**（Prepositional Phrases）

|介词短语|中文|用法示例|
|---|---|---|
|in the morning|在早上|I study in the morning.|
|at night|在夜晚|She works at night.|
|on Monday|在周一|I’ll go on Monday.|
|in 2020|在2020年|He graduated in 2020.|
|for two hours|持续两小时|We talked for two hours.|
|since 2010|自2010年起|I’ve lived here since 2010.|
|by next week|到下周为止|The work will be done by next week.|
|until tomorrow|直到明天|Wait until tomorrow.|
|after lunch|午饭之后|I’ll call you after lunch.|

---

### ④ **时间状语从句（由连词引导）**

|连词|中文|示例从句|
|---|---|---|
|when|当...时|I was sleeping **when** he came.|
|while|当...时候|**While** I was reading, the phone rang.|
|before|在...之前|Finish your work **before** you leave.|
|after|在...之后|I'll go **after** I finish dinner.|
|as soon as|一...就...|Call me **as soon as** you arrive.|
|since|自从|I haven't seen her **since** she moved.|
|until / till|直到|Stay here **until** I return.|

---

## ✅ 总结一句口诀：

> **副词短语说具体，介词连接时间里；  
> 连词引导从句时，主句时态要注意。**
