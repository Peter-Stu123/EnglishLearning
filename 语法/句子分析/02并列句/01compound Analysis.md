# 定义
- **并列句（Compound Sentence）**：由 **两个或两个以上意义相对独立、语法上完整的分句** 通过 **并列连词、分号或逗号 + 并列连词** 连接而成。
- 每个分句都可以单独成句，但在逻辑上紧密相关。
- **即使分句的谓语类型不同（系表 vs 主谓）**，只要两个分句都有主语和谓语，都可以用并列连词 **and/but/or** 连接。
	Many people are willing to fight for their dreams, and others die in their sleep.
## 两句话变成一句话的方式
1. 加连词
2. 留一个为主句，另一个为从句
3. 留一个主句作谓语，另一个为非谓语（独立主格）
## 并列句
### con.->conjuction
- **表示并列/累加**（addition）
    - **and, both…and…, not only…but also…**
    - 示例：She studies hard, **and** she always helps others.
- **表示转折/对比**（contrast）
    - **but, yet, however (句首/分号后), on the other hand，whereas**
    - 示例：He is rich, **but** he is not happy.
- **表示选择**（choice）
    - **or, either…or…, neither…nor…**
    - 示例：You can stay here, **or** you can come with us.
- **表示因果**（cause & effect）
    - **so, therefore, thus, hence**
    - 示例：It rained heavily, **so** the match was canceled.
- **表示结果/总结**（result/conclusion）
    - **for, therefore, consequently**
    - 示例：She missed the train, **therefore** she was late.
### 表示逻辑的其他词(副词adv,介词短语prep)

#### **(1) 表示因果 / 结果 Cause & Effect / Result**

| 副词           | 例句                                                  | 说明          |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| therefore    | He studied hard; **therefore**, he passed the exam. | 因此 → 连接前因后果 |
| thus         | She forgot her umbrella; **thus**, she got wet.     | 因此/于是       |
| hence        | The road was blocked; **hence**, we took a detour.  | 因此/所以       |
| consequently | He broke the law; **consequently**, he was fined.   | 结果 → 正式书面用语 |
| beause of    |                                                     |             |
| due to       |                                                     |             |
| owing to     |                                                     |             |

---

####  **(2) 表示转折 / 对比 Contrast**

|副词|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|however|The book is long; **however**, it is interesting.|但是 → 强调转折|
|nevertheless|It was raining; **nevertheless**, they went hiking.|尽管如此 → 正式书面语|
|nonetheless|He was tired; **nonetheless**, he finished the work.|尽管如此 → 同上|
|instead|I wanted tea; **instead**, I had coffee.|相反/代替|

---

#### **(3) 表示递进 / 顺承 Addition / Sequence**

|副词|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|moreover|She is smart; **moreover**, she is kind.|而且/此外 → 强调递进|
|furthermore|The plan is feasible; **furthermore**, it is cost-effective.|此外/更进一步|
|besides|He has experience; **besides**, he has great skills.|而且/此外|

---

#### **(4) 表示选择 / 条件 Choice / Condition**

|副词|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|otherwise|Hurry up, **otherwise** you will be late.|否则 → 条件暗示|
|alternatively|You can drive; **alternatively**, you can take the train.|或者/另一种选择|

---

#### **(5) 表示总结 / 强调 Conclusion / Emphasis**

|副词|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|in short|**In short**, we must take action.|简而言之|
|in fact|**In fact**, he was right all along.|事实上 → 强调信息|
|indeed|**Indeed**, this is a serious problem.|的确/确实 → 强调|

### **2️⃣ 表示逻辑关系的介词短语（Prep Phrase）**

#### **(1) 表示原因 / 原因状语**

|介词短语|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|because of / due to / owing to|The match was canceled **because of** rain.|因为… → 原因|
|as a result of|The flight was delayed **as a result of** fog.|因为… → 书面语|
|on account of|**On account of** heavy traffic, we were late.|因为…|

---

#### **(2) 表示目的 / 结果**

|介词短语|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|for the purpose of|He studies hard **for the purpose of** passing exams.|为了… → 正式|
|with the aim of|She joined the club **with the aim of** improving her skills.|以…为目的|

---

#### **(3) 表示转折 / 对比**

|介词短语|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|in contrast to / with|**In contrast to** his brother, he is very quiet.|与…相比 → 对比|
|contrary to|**Contrary to** popular belief, cats can be very loyal.|与…相反|

#### **(4) 表示条件 /假设**

|介词短语|例句|说明|
|---|---|---|
|in case of|**In case of** fire, use the stairs.|万一/如果…|
|in the event of|**In the event of** an emergency, call 911.|万一… → 正式|
