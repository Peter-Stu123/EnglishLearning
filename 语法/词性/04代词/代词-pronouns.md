# 人称代词
## 主格(作主语)
  I,you,he,she,it,we,they
 1. She is a techer.
## 宾语(作宾语) 
me,you,him,her,it,us,them
1. GIve her the book.
## 所有格(表所属)
 my,your,his,her,its,our,their
1. Our team won the game.
# 指示代词
## 指示特定事物
this,that,these,those
1. This is my bag.
# 疑问代词
## 提供具体信息
who,whom,whose,which,what
1. who is at the door
# 不定代词
## 泛指人或事物
some,any,all,none,each,both,everyone,something等
1. Everything is ready.
# 反身代词
## 强调动作作用于自身
myself-youself-himself-herself-itself-ourselves-themselves
1. She taught herself to code.
# 相互代词
## 表示相互关系
each other-one anotheer
1. They love each other.
# 关系代词
## 引导定语从句
who-whom-whose-which-that
1. The girl who won the prize is here.
